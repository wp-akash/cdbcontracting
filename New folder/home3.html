About CDB

Good construction begins with a clear brief.

Based in Business Bay, CDB Contracting LLC works across Dubai’s residential and commercial sectors. We define structural works, site works and finishes around your drawings, site conditions and target handover.

Our approach →

Define the scope early.

Review the drawings, site conditions and required finishes before pricing. Identify decisions that could affect the sequence of work or target handover.

Plan around the site.

Agree access, delivery routes, inspection points and closeout responsibilities before work starts. For occupied spaces, plan how to keep access open and limit disruption.
