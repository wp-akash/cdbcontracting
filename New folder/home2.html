Make site requirements clear.

Confirm delivery routes, work zones, risk assessments and who will record inspections. For occupied buildings, agree how access will be maintained and how noisy or dusty work will be managed.

FAQ

Before you enquire.

What should I send for a quotation?

Include the site location, proposed work, target start date and any drawings or specifications you have.

Can I discuss a project before drawings are ready?

Yes. Share a brief description and the information you have so we can identify what’s needed next.

Can I enquire about one part of a project?

Yes. Identify the works you want priced and any overlap with other trades or an existing contractor.

How are specialist methods selected?

We discuss the options against the drawings, required tolerances and site conditions before agreeing a method.

What should I flag for a renovation?

Note whether the space will be occupied, any restrictions on access or work hours, and what needs to be retained.
