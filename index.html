CDB blog

Construction insights.

Practical guides for preparing a brief, planning a fit-out and comparing construction quotations.

Residential

What to include in a villa construction brief

Give your contractor the information they need to start discussing scope and pricing.

Read the villa guide

Start with the plot location, any available drawings and your target start and handover dates. List the spaces and finishes that matter most so the team can identify decisions that affect the structure or programme.

Say whether your enquiry covers structural works, a finished villa or a turnkey package. Flag any site access constraints and note which drawings or specifications are still in progress.

Fit-out

What to check before an office fit-out

Work out how the space will be used and when contractors can access it.

Read the fit-out guide

Map the desks, meeting rooms, storage and circulation you need. Walk the site with your team and record which partitions, finishes and building services can stay.

If the office will stay open, agree work zones, delivery routes and permitted work hours with the building manager. Share those restrictions with your contractor when requesting a proposal.

Project planning

How to compare construction quotes

Check the scope, materials and assumptions behind each price.

Read the quotation guide

Send each contractor the same drawings and scope. Review which trades, finishes and site preparations are included, and ask for exclusions and provisional allowances in writing.

Compare the proposed schedule, payment milestones and handover items. Ask what would trigger a price change so you can discuss variations before agreeing the scope.
